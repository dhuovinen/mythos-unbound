The idea

Blood remembers.

Every unit knows who it is facing. Parents, children, lovers, rivals and killers all change how a unit fights, and the effects are taken straight from the myths. Pick your deck with the family tree in mind.

vs

Blood doesn't cross pantheons. Fight a different mythology and every grudge goes quiet, which can also be a deliberate strategy. Allies matter too: family fighting side by side gains Kinship, and a blessing from a parent adds damage.

How it plays

Draft the family. Then break it.

01

Pick from the family tree

Choose a pantheon and build a deck from its family tree. Your three opening picks are revealed to the opponent, who drafts blind at the same time you do.

02

Summon with faith

Faith builds up over time. Spend it to send units down the lane. You can have up to eight units on the field, but only two of them can be gods or titans.

03

Read your rival

A strategy consultant explains the board as the battle goes. Its advice uses the same reasoning your opponent uses to make its moves.

Every line on the tree is a reason to fight, or a reason not to.
Hover a deity to light up its family: who it hesitates against, who it hunts, and who it would never touch.
